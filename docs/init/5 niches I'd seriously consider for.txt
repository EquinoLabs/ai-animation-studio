5 niches I'd seriously consider for your script builder


1. Business stories & company failures
My first choice

Tell stories about brands, business failures, bizarre success stories, rivalries and corporate decisions.

Examples: How a company lost billions, why a famous brand disappeared, the business behind an everyday product.



2. Scams, fraud and consumer traps
Explain real scams, online deception, fraudulent businesses, pyramid schemes and how people get tricked.

Strong storytelling potential, curiosity-driven titles and useful content. Keep it educational and victim-sensitive.




3. History's strange true stories
Forgotten inventions, unusual historical events, mysterious expeditions, bizarre laws and forgotten civilizations.

Evergreen, suitable for long-form and Shorts, with potential for series.



4. AI and technology explained simply
Explain AI tools, emerging technologies, robotics, cybersecurity and how technology is changing jobs.

Potentially strong commercial value and sponsorship opportunities, but information becomes outdated quickly.




5. Space and science mysteries
Black holes, planetary discoveries, strange scientific experiments, space missions and scientific breakthroughs.

Highly visual, works well with narration, animation and documentary-style videos.
